# Fishing Adventure 🎣

## Game Description

Fishing Adventure is a simple browser-based fishing game where the player uses a fishing rod to catch fish that move around the game area.

The game includes two types of fish: good fish and bad fish. Catching a good fish gives the player 1 point, while catching a bad fish makes the player lose 1 life.

The player starts with 3 lives. The goal is to catch good fish, avoid bad fish, and reach 15 points to win the game.

## How to Play

1. Click the **Start Game** button to begin.
2. Use the fishing rod to catch the fish.
3. Catch a good fish to earn 1 point.
4. Avoid the bad fish because they will cost you 1 life.
5. Reach 15 points to win the game.
6. If you lose all 3 lives, the game is over.
7. Click **Play Again** to restart the game.

## Game Rules

* 🐟 Good Fish = +1 Point
* 🐡 Bad Fish = Lose 1 Life
* ❤️ Starting Lives = 3
* 🏆 15 Points = You Win!
* 💀 Lose 3 Lives = Game Over

The goal of Fishing Adventure is to catch as many good fish as possible, avoid the bad fish, and reach 15 points before losing all three lives.
